# 2.5-Minute Pitch Script

**Opening (20 seconds)**  
Good [morning/afternoon], everyone. Our research tackles a vital question for the construction industry in the Middle East and Africa: *How can renewable energy improve cost efficiency and sustainability on construction sites?*

**The Problem (30 seconds)**  
Most construction sites in the region still rely on diesel generators. These are costly, polluting, and logistically challenging. Through surveys with engineers, project managers, and contractors, we confirmed the high fuel costs, frequent maintenance issues, and concerns about sustainability.

**Our Approach (40 seconds)**  
We collected survey data from professionals across the UAE, Nigeria, Saudi Arabia, and other countries, and combined it with real generator pricing data. We then simulated a model comparing diesel and hybrid (diesel + solar) systems. The analysis showed that while diesel dominates today, hybrid systems can deliver significant long-term savings and reduce CO₂ emissions.

**Communication Strategy (40 seconds)**  
To reach our audience — contracting companies — we focused on channels they already use: WhatsApp for immediacy, Slack for structured collaboration, and Google Meet for milestone alignment. For external outreach, we propose LinkedIn and WhatsApp campaigns, supported by a one-page infographic that highlights cost comparisons, emission reductions, and practical benefits.

**Closing (30 seconds)**  
Our research shows that hybrid systems are not only viable but increasingly essential. By adopting renewables, contractors can cut costs, meet client sustainability demands, and position themselves as industry leaders. Thank you, and we look forward to engaging with stakeholders to make this transition a reality.
