# Target Audience Document

## Audience
Our primary audience consists of **contracting companies** operating in the Middle East and Africa (MEA) region.

## Capabilities
- Strong technical knowledge in construction practices.
- Familiar with conventional power solutions such as diesel generators.
- Some awareness of sustainability practices, but limited hands-on exposure to renewables.

## Constraints
- High upfront costs for renewable systems.
- Concerns around technical reliability and maintenance.
- Regulatory uncertainty depending on country and project type.

## How We Intend to Reach Them
- **Digital outreach** via LinkedIn and WhatsApp campaigns.
- **Infographics and visual summaries** that are easily shareable and digestible.
- Presence in professional construction networks.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the **cost-saving potential** of renewable energy in the medium-to-long term.
- Appreciate the **environmental and regulatory benefits** of hybrid systems.
- Realize that renewable adoption does not require a complete replacement of diesel, but can be hybridized.

## Desired Actions
- Contractors to consider including **renewable energy components** in future projects.
- Decision-makers to evaluate hybrid solutions when planning site power needs.
- Increased openness to pilot projects in collaboration with renewable suppliers.
