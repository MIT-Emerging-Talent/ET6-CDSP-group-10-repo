# 5_communication_strategy

## Channels and Usage

- **WhatsApp** — quick updates, reminders, and ad-hoc coordination among team
  members.

- **Slack** — structured threads for decisions, file sharing, and tracking
  action items.

- **Google Meet** — live discussions for alignment, weekly checkpoints, and
  milestone reviews.

## Cadence

- **WhatsApp**: Daily or as needed for rapid responses.
- **Slack**: Ongoing, with end-of-day summaries and decision logs.
- **Google Meet**: Scheduled sessions for kick-off, mid-point checks, and
  milestone readouts.

## Governance

- Major decisions and next steps are summarized in Slack after each Google
  Meet.

- WhatsApp remains for urgent notes; action items move to Slack for
  visibility.

- Files, datasets, and notebooks are stored in the GitHub repository; links
  are shared in Slack.

## Challenges and Mitigations

- **Fragmentation across channels** → Consolidate final decisions in Slack
  threads and reference in the README.

- **Response delays** → Assign clear owners per task; use Slack mentions for
  blockers.

- **Uneven contributions** → Establish weekly checkpoint on Google Meet with
  clear deliverables.

## Outcome

A simple, multi-channel setup balancing speed (WhatsApp), organization (Slack),
and alignment (Google Meet).
